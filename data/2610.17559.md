**Figure 3. -** Radio cutouts corresponding to the ten GHz measurements in Table tab:photometry, ordered by observing date. Top row: FIRST ($1.435$ GHz), VLASS 1.2 ($3$ GHz), RACS-mid ($1.367$ GHz), VLASS 2.2 ($3$ GHz), and RACS-high ($1.655$ GHz). Bottom row: RACS-low ($0.887$ GHz), the VAST-low stack ($0.943$ GHz), VLASS 3.2 ($3$ GHz), and VAST-mid at $1.367$ GHz on 2024-10-06 and 2024-12-13. The white circle marks the Gaia DR3 position used for the forced fits and has radius $8\arcsec$. The number in each panel is the map intensity at that position in $\mathrm{mJy\,beam^{-1}}$, shown for visual reference. These values are not the fitted flux densities listed in Table tab:photometry. Each panel uses an independent linear display range. All panels are $60\arcsec$ on a side. Grey ellipses mark the restoring beams. (*fig:cutouts*)
