<div id="title" markdown="1">

# Large VLA Observations Confirm AT 2023mfm as an Off-nuclear Tidal Disruption Event

</div>
<div id="authors" markdown="1">

Wenkai Li, et al.

</div>
<div id="abstract" markdown="1">

**Abstract:** We report new radio observations of the tidal disruption event (TDE) AT 2023mfm, which we identified as a high-confidence candidate in a systematic search for off-nuclear TDEs. High-resolution NSF Karl G. Jansky Very Large Array C-band (6 GHz) imaging resolves two radio sources: one consistent with the host-galaxy nucleus and one offset by $0.651\pm 0.036"$ ( $1.06\pm0.06$ kpc), consistent with the Zwicky Transient Facility and Pan-STARRS1 positions of AT 2023mfm. These observations confirm the off-nuclear nature of AT 2023mfm, demonstrating the power of high-resolution radio imaging to validate off-nuclear TDE candidates and reveal hidden off-nuclear massive black holes.

</div>
